ABOUT ME

Knowledge Management
across the enterprise

I am a Knowledge Management professional focused on designing structured, searchable and governed knowledge experiences for enterprise HR environments.

My work spans the complete knowledge lifecycle — from content strategy, information architecture and taxonomy through governance, publishing, analytics, migration and continuous improvement.

I design knowledge for two key audiences: employees who need simple and actionable self-service information, and HRSS agents who need detailed, reliable knowledge to resolve employee cases efficiently and consistently.
